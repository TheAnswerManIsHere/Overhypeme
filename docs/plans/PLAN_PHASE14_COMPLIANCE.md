## Data Model and Migration Impact

Three migrations, in increments 4, 5 and 7, each reviewed under the migration skill and serialized after Issue #1018's.

- **Acceptance record (increment 4).** New table: account (cascade on account deletion), document kind, version, accepted-at, and for a photo confirmation the upload it covers. Append-only by convention and by having no update path in the code; not a database-enforced boundary, and said so. No backfill: no existing account has accepted anything, which is the correct starting state (they are asked once). Row states: none / accepted an old version / accepted the current version — the gate reads only "has a row at the current version".
- **Photo confirmation (increment 5).** Recorded in the same table against the upload. Existing uploads have no row, which is the "must confirm before reuse" state; no backfill.

## Runtime Behavior

## Risks and Mitigations

- **Geolocation is approximate, and Crimea may geolocate as Russia or Ukraine.** The region check reads Cloudflare's data and cannot be better than it. Mitigation: the Terms carry the member's representation that they are not in a sanctioned place; the residue goes to counsel.
- **The origin secret can lock the site out** if the Cloudflare rule is removed or mistyped. Mitigation: deployed in two steps — observe (log requests lacking it) for a day, then enforce; the rollback is one environment variable.
- **The engine promises may not hold for every engine.** If an engine in use is excluded from fal's no-training promise, the options are to stop sending member photos to it or to say so in Privacy; that is a product consequence and comes to David when the retention increment reads the actual list.
- **NCMEC may require the file.** Then S-f is wrong and the smallest correct change is wiring the already-built upload client for a human-triggered send; that is a plan amendment David approves.
- **Texts are not legal advice.** They are drafted from the research and marked as awaiting counsel; counsel may change them, and a changed version re-asks every account.
- **Sequencing behind Issue #1018** may delay increments 4–7; increments 1–3 and 8's drafting do not wait.
