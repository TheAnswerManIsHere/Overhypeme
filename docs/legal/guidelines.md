Overhype.me is for over-the-top praise: facts so exaggerated that nobody could believe them, made about yourself or the people you like. These guidelines explain what fits. They are part of our [Terms](/terms).

## The spirit of it

- **Hyperbole, not claims.** A good fact is impossible on its face. "David once out-stared the sun" is a fact. "David stole from his employer" is an accusation, and it does not belong here, even as a joke.
- **Praise, not put-downs.** Facts make their subject look legendary. Content meant to mock, shame or hurt a real person does not fit, however funny it seems.
- **Everyone is a grown-up here.** Overhype.me is for adults, but it is not an adult site.

## Not allowed

- **Anything sexual involving a minor, in any form, real or generated.** We remove it, end the account, and report it to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children.
- **Sexual or nude content of anyone**, and any intimate image of a real person shared without their consent.
- **Pictures of other people without their permission.** Upload only photos of yourself.
- **Hate**: attacks on people for their race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, caste, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability or serious illness.
- **Threats, harassment and bullying**, including encouraging others to target someone.
- **Violence and self-harm**: praising or encouraging either.
- **Private information**: someone's address, phone number, documents or anything else they have not made public.
- **Deception**: pictures made to be mistaken for real photographs of real events, impersonating someone, or presenting a fact as true.
- **Defamation**: statements a reasonable person could take as true that would damage a real person's reputation.
- **Illegal goods and activity**, and content that infringes copyright or trademarks (see our [Copyright Policy](/copyright)).
- **Spam and manipulation**: repetitive posting, fake votes, misuse of share-by-email, or anything that games the rankings.

## How we enforce them

Submitted facts are reviewed before they appear. We remove content that breaks these guidelines, and depending on how serious and how repeated the problem is, we may limit, suspend or end an account. Child sexual abuse material and credible threats lead to immediate removal and the account being ended.

## Reporting a problem

Until reporting buttons are added to the site, write to us:

- **A picture of you made or shared without your permission**, including an intimate image: legal@overhype.me, with a link to it and a sentence saying it shows you. Say if it is intimate or sexual; we handle those first.
- **Copyright**: follow our [Copyright Policy](/copyright).
- **Anything else** that breaks these guidelines: support@overhype.me, with a link.
