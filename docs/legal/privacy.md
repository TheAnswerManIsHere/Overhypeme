This policy explains how **Availeron Consulting, Inc.**, 745 5th Ave., Suite 500, New York, NY 10151, United States ("we", "us"), handles personal information on Overhype.me. Questions and requests: privacy@overhype.me.

Overhype.me is offered only in the places listed in our [Terms](/terms) and only to adults. It is not offered in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom.

## What we collect

**When you browse, signed in or not**

- **Technical information**: your IP address, browser and device type, the pages you visit, and when. Our hosting and security providers process this to deliver and protect the site.
- **How you use the site.** Our analytics providers, Google Analytics and PostHog, receive the pages you visit and the steps you take, such as personalising a fact, making a meme or signing up, with a random identifier and, if you are signed in, your account ID. They do not receive your name, your email address, the names you type or what you search for. Our error-monitoring provider receives error details and page-speed measurements. A notice tells you this the first time you visit.
- **Your approximate location** (country), worked out from your IP address by our network provider. We use it to decide which marketing-email default to show you and, as described in our Terms, where the Service is offered.
- **Names you type to personalise facts** are kept in your browser, and sent to our servers when they are needed to show or make something with them.

**When you create an account**

- Your email address and password (stored only as a one-way hash), or, if you sign in with Google or Apple, the email address and name they share with us.
- Your date of birth, the name and pronouns you choose, and your marketing-email choice, with the date you made it and the wording you were shown.
- Your acceptance of these policies, when we start recording it.

**When you use the Service**

- **Content you create**: facts, comments, votes, reactions, the memes and pictures you make, and searches you run.
- **Photos you upload.** A photo is a picture of your face. We use it only to make the pictures and memes you ask for, and to show it as your profile photo if you choose.
- **Payments**: if you buy a membership, Stripe collects your card details directly; we receive your Stripe customer reference, what you bought and its status, never your full card number.
- **Messages you send us**, and records of reports and safety reviews.

## How we use it

To run the Service and your account; to make the pictures you ask for; to moderate content and keep people safe; to process payments; to send you emails about your account and, if you agreed, news about the site; to prevent abuse and fraud; to fix problems; and to meet our legal obligations, including reporting apparent child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children.

**We do not sell your personal information**, and we do not share it for advertising targeted on other sites.

**Photos.** We check that a photo shows a face, but we do not build face templates from it, recognise you, or identify anyone. We do not use your photos to train artificial-intelligence models. fal.ai's terms say it will not use what we send it to train models, apart from models it marks as excluded; we are checking the models we use against that list and will update this policy with the result.

## Who receives it

We share personal information only with the providers that run parts of the Service for us, and only what each needs:

| Provider | What it does | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| Replit (with Google Cloud) | Hosts the site, database and stored files | Everything the Service stores |
| Cloudflare | Network, security, bot checks | Your IP address and requests |
| fal.ai and the image models it runs, which come from several AI companies | Checks uploaded photos for safety; makes AI pictures | Your photo, for the safety check at upload; the prompt for each picture it makes |
| OpenAI | Writing aids and content checks | Fact and comment text, a first name when suggesting pronouns, and in some checks a photo |
| Project Arachnid (Canadian Centre for Child Protection) | Detects known child sexual abuse material | Uploaded images |
| Stripe | Payments | Your email, account reference and purchases |
| Resend | Sends email | Your email address and the message |
| Sentry | Finds and diagnoses errors in the site | Error details and page-speed measurements; for signed-in members, your account ID; and anything you send through its feedback button |
| Google Analytics | Counts visits and the steps visitors take | Your IP address, pages visited, steps taken and device information, with a random identifier and, for signed-in members, your account ID |
| PostHog | Shows how visitors move through the site | Your IP address, pages visited, steps taken and device information, with a random identifier and, for signed-in members, your account ID |
| Google and Apple | Sign-in, if you choose them | What you approve in their sign-in screen |

We also disclose information when the law requires it, to protect people from harm, in connection with a sale or reorganisation of the business, or with your permission.

**Sharing by email.** If you share something by email, we send it to the address you enter. We use that address only to send that one message and to limit abuse of the feature.

## Your choices and rights

- **Delete your account** from your profile at any time.
- **Delete any photo, meme or picture** from "My content".
- **Marketing emails**: you choose at sign-up. Today, Legendary members also receive a daily "Fact of the Day" email whatever they chose, without an unsubscribe link; we are changing it so it goes only to members who agreed and carries an unsubscribe link. Until then, write to support@overhype.me to stop it.
- **Ask us** to tell you what we hold about you or to correct it: write to privacy@overhype.me. We will verify that the request comes from you and answer within the time the law of your state requires.
- **California residents**: you have the rights above. We do not sell or share personal information as California law defines those terms. You will not be treated differently for asking about your information.

**Do Not Track.** Browsers' "Do Not Track" signals are not a standard we can act on, so we do not respond to them.

## Children

Overhype.me is for adults and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, write to privacy@overhype.me and we will delete it.

## Security

We protect information with encryption in transit, access controls and limited staff access, but no service is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you as the law requires.

## Changes
