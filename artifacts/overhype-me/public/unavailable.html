We don't offer Overhype.me in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or places under broad US sanctions (Cuba, Iran, North Korea, and the Crimea, Donetsk and Luhansk regions of Ukraine).

We decide this from the location of your internet connection, which isn't always exact. If you think we've got it wrong, write to support@overhype.me.
